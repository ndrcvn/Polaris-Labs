We are a San Francisco–based architecture and design studio. We work on buildings, interiors, and the space between.

Palazzo Projects was founded on a simple premise: that good design should be quiet. Not silent — but considered. Every project we take on begins with a long conversation and ends with something that feels like it could not have been any other way.

We are a small practice by intention. We work with a limited number of clients each year to ensure that each project receives our full attention from concept through completion.

Our work spans new construction, adaptive reuse, and interior design — across residential and commercial commissions throughout California and beyond.
