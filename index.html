The Studio
Good design is
never
accidental.

Palazzo Projects is an architecture and interior design studio founded on the belief that the spaces we inhabit shape who we are. We work with a small roster of clients each year to ensure that every project receives our full attention.

Our work spans private residences, boutique hospitality, and cultural institutions — united by a commitment to craft, material honesty, and spaces that reward slow looking.

How We Work

Process & method.

01

Discovery

We begin by listening. Understanding how you live, work, and move through space is the foundation of every decision we make.

02

Concept

A clear spatial narrative emerges — sketched, modelled, and refined until the idea is precise enough to build from.

03

Development

Every detail is resolved: material palettes, bespoke joinery, lighting schemes, and contractor specifications.

04

Delivery

We remain on site until the final lamp is placed — ensuring the built reality matches the vision without compromise.
